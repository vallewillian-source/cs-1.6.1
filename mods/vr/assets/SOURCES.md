  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wwwriks/wrad-arms/main/arms.glb`
- License: CC0 1.0 (`LICENSE` in this folder; the GitHub API confirms `spdx_id = CC0-1.0`)
- Files used: `arms.glb` (mesh+skeleton+embedded texture),
  `arm_albedo_pale.png` (512×512 RGBA) and `arm_albedo_dark.png` (2nd skin, optional)
- **Rig report** (generated by `../tools/glb_inspect.py assets/arms.glb`):
  - 1 mesh: **872 vertices, 1196 triangles**; attributes
    `POSITION/NORMAL/TEXCOORD_0/JOINTS_0/WEIGHTS_0` → **skinned mesh** ✓
  - **PNG texture embedded in the GLB** (`images[0] = arm_albedo_pale`, bufferView 6)
  - `skin[0]` with **50 bones**: `root`, `shoulder.{r,l}`, `bicep`, `forearm`, `wrist`,
    `finger_{pinky,ring,middle,index,thumb}{1,2,3}`, **`socket.{r,l}`**,
    `forearm.Twist{0,1}`, **`wrist_ik.{r,l}`**, **`arm_target.{r,l}`**, `head`
- **`socket.r` is the rig's weapon fit** — **it is no longer used** (V2 mounts the weapon
  in the HAND frame, derived from the bones themselves; see below)
  - **`wrist_ik` / `arm_target`** show that the rig was built for IK
  - Default pose **is not a T-pose**: the arms already come in a natural carry pose
  - local bbox ≈ **10.97 × 4.93 × 4.41** units → **non-metric scale** (calibrate via cfg)
  - **0 animations** (just the rig)
  - `head` at (0, +1.5, 0) and `root` at (0, −1, 0) → there is a body frame in the rig

### Palm frame (measured from the rig, on load)

The code derives, per hand, from the rig's rest pose:
`palma_frente` = wrist → average of the bases of the 4 long fingers; `palma_normal` =
`cross(frente, pinky−index)`, with the sign chosen to point to the centre of the rig (a hand's palm
in a natural position faces the body); `palma_centro` = wrist + 55% of that
path. Measurements from `arms.glb`:

| hand | palma_frente | palma_normal | palm centre |
|---|---|---|---|
| left | (−0.71 −0.49 −0.50) | (−0.27 +0.85 −0.44) | 0.37 rig units from the wrist |
| right | (+0.71 −0.49 −0.50) | (+0.27 +0.85 −0.44) | 0.37 rig units from the wrist |

(≈ 1.6 GoldSrc units = 4 cm from the wrist, already with `scale_arms`.) It is this point that
the weapon grip anchors to, and it is what made the fit stop being a guess.

## Pistol — `Pistol_1.obj` (Quaternius, *Ultimate Guns Pack*)

- Source: **OpenGameArt, upload by Quaternius himself** —
  <https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack> →
  `ultimate_gun_pack_by_quaternius.zip` (7.4 MB)
- License: **CC0** (declared on the OGA page: *"This pack contains 40 different gun
  models. All in FBX, OBJ and Blend formats, free to use in personal and commercial
  projects."*)
- The same pack also exists on `quaternius.com` (Google Drive) and `poly.pizza`. On the day of
  acquisition the Drive answered **"quota exceeded"** and the poly.pizza API requires a key — hence
  OpenGameArt, which has a direct link and is the author's upload.
- Files used: `Pistol_1.obj` + `Pistol_1.mtl` (the pack also brings `Pistol_2..6`)
- Geometry: **580 vertices, 487 faces**, **normals YES, UVs NO** (`vt=0`)
- Colour: **5 materials by `Kd`** (`Black`, `Black2`, `DarkMetal`, `DarkWood`, `Metal`) —
  **no texture at all** (the zip brings no image other than `Preview.jpg`)
- Scale: ≈ **1.82 × 1.16 × 0.32** units — same order of magnitude as the arms GLB
- Switching pistol = swapping the `.obj/.mtl` pair (and the name in the cfg)

## Calibration notes (for Phase 2)

- Neither of the two assets is metric → the real scale goes in the cfg, not in the code.
- The MTL's `Kd` is **dark and linear** (e.g. `Metal` = 0.0533): convert to sRGB when
  using it as a colour (0.0533 → ≈ 0.26 grey), otherwise the weapon comes out almost black.
- The pistol **has no UVs**, so it is drawn with **solid colour per material**; the
  arms are textured. So that the weapon does not look flat, apply simple shading
  with the normal (`N·L` from a fixed light next to the camera).
